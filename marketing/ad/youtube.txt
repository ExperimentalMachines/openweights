TITLE
OpenWeights: open-weight AI that runs on your phone

DESCRIPTION
Some things shouldn't leave your phone. OpenWeights brings the AI to them.

OpenWeights is a free, open-source Android app that runs open-weight AI models on the phone itself. No account, no analytics.

• Runs on the phone's own chip: the CPU, the GPU, and the NPU on Snapdragon 8 Elite and MediaTek Dimensity 9500 phones. In the video a Snapdragon 8 Elite reads a 398-token lease on its NPU in 0.23 s and answers at 31 tokens a second (Qwen3-1.7B, the runtime's own figures for that reply).
• The tools that go online are named "Leaves the device", each with its own switch. Model search and downloads use the internet; the privacy policy says exactly what leaves and when.
• Hugging Face is the model list: search it in the app and get an estimate of whether each file fits your phone before you download it.
• Optional agent tools: a folder you share, sandboxed JavaScript, memory, and a Canvas where the model builds a page, a document or a slide deck. Saving waits for your OK in the mode shown.

Everything in the video is real: the phones' own screen recordings on a Snapdragon 8 Elite (Qualcomm Device Cloud) and a POCO X8 Pro Max, on the build released on Google Play. Nothing is sped up; the one cut, a minute while the model writes the page, is labelled. Models can make mistakes: the lease answer is the model's, unchecked.
